# P53.1 — Alpha Flow Hardening Audit

## Intent

Prove that the completed vertical slice still behaves as one coherent product when accessibility, controller focus, persistence, audio feedback, responsive layout, and the full three-phase scenario flow interact.

## Audit contract

- Exercise Main Menu → War Council → Preparation → Battle → Recovery → authored decision → final Battle → Results through the UI controller.
- Run the journey at 2560×1440 with 150% UI scale, high contrast, reduced motion, and muted audio.
- Treat focus ownership as the controller reachability contract at every primary transition.
- Save and reload both an ordinary recovery lull and the open Twilight Crossroads decision through isolated files.
- Require semantic cue IDs and visible cue labels even when audio output is muted.
- Require terminal Results, seed pressure, recovery mastery, and the replay action to remain visible and horizontally contained.

## Authority boundary

The audit may call existing UI commands and inspect presentation state, but it adds no simulation rule, save field, settings field, or release claim. `PackKeepState`, the existing persistence paths, and the semantic cue service remain authoritative.

## Acceptance

- Accessibility changes never mutate the authoritative run.
- Save/load restores equivalent authoritative state at both recovery boundaries.
- Controller-first focus reaches setup, assault readiness, authored choice, recovery continuation, and terminal replay.
- Muted/reduced-motion play still reports semantic battle and outcome cues visibly.
- The seeded final composition and preparation focus survive the complete journey.
- The existing complete verification wrapper and packaged release gates remain green.
