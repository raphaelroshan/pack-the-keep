# K5 — Recovery and Results distinction

## Purpose

Make the post-assault chapters answer different player questions. Recovery is a constrained choice about what survives the next pressure. Terminal Results is an explanation of why the complete defense ended as it did and what to change on replay.

## Recovery contract

- Keep the exact two-action budget, material costs, blocking reasons, and authoritative commands.
- Name one first priority and one concrete alternative that receives fewer remaining actions if the priority is chosen.
- Keep forecast, consequence, and action cards on one read-only snapshot.
- Never auto-select or auto-execute the recommendation.

## Results contract

- Lead the causal section with one decisive success and one remaining cost derived from the deterministic report.
- Preserve the complete phase timeline, persistent room and defender damage, event/regional consequences, resources, and run-specific replay experiment.
- Keep terminal actions fixed and separate from Recovery commands.

## Acceptance

1. Recovery explicitly names a first priority, sacrifice, and reason.
2. The sacrifice updates after an action and never claims an action remains when the budget is exhausted.
3. Terminal Results includes a concise decisive-pattern summary derived from report facts.
4. Recovery controls never appear in terminal Results, and terminal controls never replace an inter-wave lull.
5. Save/load derives identical Recovery and Results presentation.
6. Snapshot construction remains deterministic and non-mutating.
