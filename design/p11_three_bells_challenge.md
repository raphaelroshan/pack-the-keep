# P11 — Three Bells at Dusk Challenge

## Intent

Prove that the three P11 teaching pairs compose without creating hidden rule conflicts or one mandatory pack. The scenario should ask the player to preserve answers to armor, disrupted warning, and protection-piercing pressure across one three-wave run.

## Scenario sequence

1. Shielded Advance combines a Shield Guard with an Ash Slinger, testing precision and signal redundancy together.
2. Smoke and Signal combines an Ash Slinger with a Shieldbreaker, testing warning and a preserved secondary line.
3. Break the Line combines all three P11 enemies as a final readable composition check.

## Acceptance criteria

- The scenario uses only existing P11 enemies and doctrines; it adds no one-off combat rule.
- Crossbow Watch + Bell Guard and Shieldwall + Bell Guard are both legal two-pack preparations.
- Both loadouts complete all three waves for both commanders across the bounded seed matrix without collapse.
- Same-seed command sequences serialize identically.
- Preparation, Battle, and Results identify the challenge and its mixed enemy roster.
- Existing scenario baselines remain unchanged.

## Non-goals

- No reward track, score multiplier, new enemy, or third pack opening.
- No assumption that one tested layout is optimal.
