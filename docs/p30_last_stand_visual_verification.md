# P30 Last Stand Visual Verification

- Build: `0.19.0-last-stand`
- Screen: Main Menu → Custom Defense → Briefing

Confirm the briefing offers Previous and Next controls alongside the scenario
dropdown. Select **The Last Bell** and verify the card shows `10/10`,
`OVERWHELMING`, a seven-attacker peak, the mixed enemy roster, all three pressure
phases, Shieldwall + Crossbow Watch, and the warning **Defender wipe ends the
run** without crowding the entry button.

Enter the keep with the recommended Pike Squad and Narrow Gate, then start the
assault. Let it run without commander intervention. Confirm attackers visibly
damage both health bars, the battle ends when the final defender reaches zero,
no recovery cards appear, and Results explicitly says every defender was
disabled. Restarting should create a fresh run; continuing to a second wave
must not be offered after collapse.
