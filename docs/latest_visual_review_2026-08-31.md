## Findings

The War Council clearly identifies Commander 3/3, the Quartermaster's reserve doctrine, first strategic question, and low-opening-resource limitation. Preparation shows the effective two-material first-pack cost and names Measured Stores before purchase. The fortress remains the visual anchor, while the Battle command rail identifies Resupply and explains when it is unavailable.

At 1280×720 and large text, optional detail requires vertical scrolling by design; the primary commitment, assault, and pack-opening actions remain reachable and there is no horizontal clipping. At 2560×1440 / 150%, text and board geometry remain readable. The reused tinted Castellan portrait is intentionally placeholder art and is the clearest remaining visual limitation for this commander slice.

## Next visual target

P51.2 should establish a third defensive identity with an authored silhouette and material treatment distinct from both Greywatch and Ash Ford before new enemy families are combined into it.
