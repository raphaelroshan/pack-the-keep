# PTK-EA-4 — Enemy and Counterplay Verification

The Battering Ram is an armored room destroyer with a slow demolition cadence and explicit Gate, Armory, and Workshop targets. Mason Crew pierces its armor; River Wardens and structural screens provide alternative mitigation.

The Harrier is a ranged unit hunter. It chooses the eligible ammunition user with the lowest current ammunition ratio, then lowest condition and stable instance ID. Ridge Company, Ferry Network, and mobile reserves provide distinct answers.

Both enemies have authored 32px silhouettes and reuse the established melee/ranged/demolition effect and semantic-audio vocabulary. `tests/test_early_access_campaign.gd` covers targeting, armor counterplay, asset resolution, and save boundaries; the all-scenario matrix covers their complete authored assaults.
