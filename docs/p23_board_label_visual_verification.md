# P23 Board Label Visual Verification

## Capture reviewed

A 1600×900 Three Bells at Dusk battle capture placed Pike Squad in the Gate and support/ranged pieces across the upper posts.

## Result

- Pike Squad owns the foreground label within the occupied Gate footprint; the Gate boundary and condition bar remain visible without competing room text.
- Long labels such as Crossbow Patrol and room names compact to measured cell width instead of spilling into adjacent spaces.
- Unoccupied rooms retain recognizable fitted names and numeric state where space permits.
- Full piece and room details remain available in the command-rail inspector.
- The approach apron and six-tick timeline remain separated and readable.

The PNG was a temporary local verification artifact and is intentionally not committed.
