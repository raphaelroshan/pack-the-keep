## Investment assessment

Pack the Keep currently offers the most immediately legible investment vertical. Its creative hook is visible, its tactical grammar is distinct, and its preparation-to-battle loop is demonstrable. The next investment test is whether the same quality survives a second keep and a second commander lens without turning into reskinned content or a fixed build order.

The full creative vertical should be a complete Greywatch run with a named commander, authored keep identity, pack doctrine, readable three-wave assault, one intervention, recoverable damage, and a causal Results screen. Beyond that, one alternate keep must change the spatial question and one additional commander must change the preferred doctrine or intervention pattern.

The complete implementation contract is [`investment_evaluation_roadmap.md`](investment_evaluation_roadmap.md). The first task is **PTK-I1: lock Greywatch as the creative vertical**, then proceed to the second keep only after two viable seeded plans and the full Preparation → Battle → Recovery → Results evidence are stable.
