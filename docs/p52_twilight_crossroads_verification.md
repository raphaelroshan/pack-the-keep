# P52.1 Twilight Crossroads Verification

**Build:** `0.36.0-twilight-crossroads`

## Implemented contract

- The Twilight Road opens **One Road Before Nightfall** after phase two.
- Replanting road stakes or carrying lamp oil costs exactly one of the two recovery actions.
- Stakes delay every Outrider in the combined final phase; lamps reveal every Gloam Knife.
- The preparation is consumed when phase three starts, while spawned enemy state remains authoritative and save-safe.
- Road Wardens plus Crossbow Watch and Lantern Watch plus Runner Network are deterministic mixed-pack answers when paired with the complementary recovery choice.
- The event leads the Recovery command rail, uses game-facing language, and receives first controller focus without adding a permanent HUD layer.
