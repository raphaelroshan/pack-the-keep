The right command rail now leads with **Choose What Survives** and retains the exact cost, benefit, trade-off, READY/BLOCKED state, and existing authoritative command for every recovery option. The large **End Lull — Release Phase** action remains visually dominant without obscuring the keep.

At 125% scale the keep and command rail stack, while the three-column recovery brief remains readable across the fixed 810-pixel gameplay surface. Controller focus selects the first legal recovery command. Saving and loading during the lull derives the same brief from authoritative state, and terminal Results continues to use only the dedicated debrief.

Human comprehension of the priority language and the perceived weight of the two-action trade-off remain pending structured playtest evidence.
